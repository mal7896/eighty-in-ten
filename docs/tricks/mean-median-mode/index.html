Mean, median, mode

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Averages and middles of short lists.

How to do it

  1. Mean: total ÷ count. A missing value is (count × mean) minus the rest.
  2. Median: sort, then take the middle, or average the two middles.
  3. Mode: the value that shows up most. Range: largest minus smallest.

Worked example

The average of 31, 17, 20, and k is 25. Find k⁠.
  1. Total must be 4×25=100⁠.
  2. 100−68= 32
Why it worksThe mean times the count is always the total.
Watch outSort before you look for the median.

Try these

  1. The arithmetic mean of 4, 5, 1, 8, 5 is
    Show the answer

    453​ or 523​ or 4.6

    1. Add them: 4+5+1+8+5=23⁠.
    2. Divide by 5: 453​
  2. The arithmetic mean of 5, 5, 12, 3, 3 is
    Show the answer

    553​ or 528​ or 5.6

    1. Add them: 5+5+12+3+3=28⁠.
    2. Divide by 5: 553​
  3. The average of 31, 37, 43, and k is 39. Find k⁠.
    Show the answer

    45

    1. Total must be 4×39=156⁠.
    2. 156−111= 45
  4. The sum of the median, the mode and the range of 4, 4, 6, 6, 1, 6, 6, 6 is
    Show the answer

    17

    1. In order: 1, 4, 4, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6.
    2. Median 6+ mode 6+ range 5= 17
