Roman numerals

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Reading them as ordinary numbers, and doing arithmetic with them.

How to do it

  1. I 1, V 5, X 10, L 50, C 100, D 500, M 1000.
  2. A smaller numeral before a larger one subtracts: IV 4, IX 9, XL 40, XC 90, CD 400, CM 900.
  3. Convert one place at a time: 1994= M CM XC IV.

Worked example

DCXXV = (Arabic numeral)
  1. Split into place values: 600= DC, 20= XX, 5= V.
  2. Total: 625
Why it worksNo numeral repeats more than three times in a row.
Watch out49 is XLIX, not IL.

Try these

  1. CMLXIII = (Arabic numeral)
    Show the answer

    963

    1. Split into place values: 900= CM, 60= LX, 3= III.
    2. Total: 963
  2. MMCLXV = (Arabic numeral)
    Show the answer

    2165

    1. Split into place values: 2000= MM, 100= C, 60= LX, 5= V.
    2. Total: 2165
  3. MCLXVIII − CDLXXII = (Arabic numeral)
    Show the answer

    696

    1. MCLXVIII =1168, CDLXXII =472⁠.
    2. 1168−472= 696
  4. MMDCCCLXXI = (Arabic numeral)
    Show the answer

    2871

    1. Split into place values: 2000= MM, 800= DCCC, 70= LXX, 1= I.
    2. Total: 2871
