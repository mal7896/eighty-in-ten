Greatest integer, floor and ceiling

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: [[x]] or ⌊x⌋⁠, the greatest integer less than or equal to x⁠, and the ceiling ⌈x⌉⁠.

How to do it

  1. Positive numbers: drop the decimal.
  2. Negative numbers: go down to the next integer. [[−2.3]]=−3⁠.
  3. Roots inside: estimate each to two decimals. ⌊10​+7​⌋⁠: 3.16+2.65=5.81⁠, so 5. The ceiling ⌈x⌉ rounds up instead.

Worked example

[[−6.3]]+[[4]]=
  1. [[x]] is the greatest integer ≤x⁠. For negatives that means going down: [[−6.3]]=−7,[[4]]=4⁠.
  2. Sum: −3
Why it works"Less than or equal" means moving left on the number line.
Watch outNegatives are the whole trap.

    2. Round up: 9

    Show the answer

    1. 2​≈1.41,3​≈1.73,5​≈2.24,7​≈2.65⁠. The total is about 8.03.
    2. Round down: 8

    Show the answer

    2. Sum: 6
