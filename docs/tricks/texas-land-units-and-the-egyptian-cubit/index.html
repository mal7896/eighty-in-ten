Texas land units and the Egyptian cubit

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: Varas, leagues and labors of land, and Egyptian cubits. They show up on starred problems.

How to do it

  1. A Texas vara is 3331​ inches, so 27 varas =25 yards. Varas to yards: multiply by about .926. Yards to varas: multiply by 1.08.
  2. A league of land is 25,000,000 square varas, about 4428 acres. A labor is 1,000,000 square varas, about 177 acres, so a league is 25 labors.
  3. An Egyptian cubit is about 17.72 inches. That's the value recent UIL answer keys work out to.
  4. These are starred, so you only need to land within 5%. Round the conversion and keep the other number exact.

Worked example

* 21 leagues of land in Texas minus 24 labors of land in Texas is acres
  1. A league of land is about 4428 acres and a labor is about 177 acres (a league is 25 labors). A square vara is tiny, about 1/5600 of an acre.
  2. 21×4428−24×177≈88,745

Answer: 84,308 to 93,182

Why it worksA square vara is (3331​)2≈1111 square inches, and an acre is 6,272,640 square inches.
Watch outLeagues and labors of land are areas (acres), not lengths.
