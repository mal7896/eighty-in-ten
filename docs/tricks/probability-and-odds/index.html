Probability and odds

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Dice, coins and marbles.

How to do it

  1. Probability = favorable ÷ total.
  2. Two dice: sums 2 through 12 happen 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 ways out of 36.
  3. Odds in favor = favorable : unfavorable.
  4. Cards: count, and don't count the overlap twice. A heart or a face card is 13+12−3=22 cards.
  5. After one draw without replacement, the second draw is from one fewer, and the first color has one fewer.
  6. Odds of losing a:b means the chance of winning is a+bb​⁠.
  7. Two 4-sided dice: sums 2 to 8 happen 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1 ways out of 16.

Worked example

One card is drawn from a standard deck. The probability that it is a heart or a face card is
  1. Count the cards, and don't count the overlap twice: 22 cards.
  2. 22/52= 2611​
Why it worksOdds compare the two outcomes to each other, not to the total.
Watch outWithout replacement, the second draw has one fewer marble.

Try these

  1. Two dice are rolled. The probability of a sum of 6 or 9 is
    Show the answer

    41​ or .25

    1. 6: 5 ways; 9: 4 ways.
    2. 9 out of 36:9/36= 41​
  2. A coin is tossed 5 times. The probability of exactly 3 heads is
    Show the answer

    165​ or .3125

    1. (35​)=10 arrangements out of 25=32⁠.
    2. 165​
  3. A bag has 4 red, 6 blue, and 5 green marbles. The odds in favor of drawing a red marble are
    Show the answer

    4:11

    1. Odds in favor = favorable : unfavorable =4:11⁠.
    2. Reduced: 4 : 11
  4. One card is drawn from a standard deck. The probability that it is a heart or a face card is
    Show the answer

    2611​

    1. Count the cards, and don't count the overlap twice: 22 cards.
    2. 22/52= 2611​
