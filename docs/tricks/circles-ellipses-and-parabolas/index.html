Circles, ellipses and parabolas

  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Radius of a circle in general form, area of an ellipse, focus and directrix of a parabola.

How to do it

  1. x2+y2+Dx+Ey+F=0 has center (−2D​,−2E​) and r2=(2D​)2+(2E​)2−F⁠.
  2. a2x2​+b2y2​=1 has area πab⁠.
  3. Parabola y=ax2+⋯⁠: the focus and directrix are p=4a1​ above and below the vertex. For x=ay2 they're left and right, so the directrix of x=y2 is x=−41​⁠.

Worked example

The radius of the circle x2+y2−10x+24y=0 is
  1. Center (5,−12)⁠: the opposites of half the x and y coefficients. r2=25+144−0=169⁠.
  2. r= 13
Why it worksBoth come from completing the square.
Watch outGet the ellipse into "=1" form before reading off a and b⁠.
