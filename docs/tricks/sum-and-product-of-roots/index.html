Sum and product of roots

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: Questions about the roots of ax2+bx+c=0 (or a cubic).

How to do it

  1. Sum =−ab​⁠. Product =ac​⁠.
  2. Discriminant: b2−4ac⁠.
  3. Sum of the squares of the roots: (sum)2−2(product).
  4. Cubic ax3+bx2+cx+d⁠: sum −ab​⁠, product −ad​⁠.
  5. Cubic, with pairs =PQ+QR+RP=ac​⁠: (P+Q)(Q+R)(R+P)= sum × pairs − product. The harmonic mean of the roots is pairs3⋅product​⁠.
  6. Combinations: (P+1)(Q+1)=PQ+(P+Q)+1⁠. P2+kPQ+Q2=(P+Q)2+(k−2)PQ⁠. Arithmetic mean 2P+Q​⁠, harmonic mean P+Q2PQ​⁠.
  7. a(bx−c)2=d⁠: square-root first, bx−c=±d/a​⁠. If f(x) has roots m and n⁠, then f(x−t) has roots m+t and n+t⁠.

Worked example

P, Q and R are the roots of x3+x2−22x−40=0⁠. Find (P + Q)(Q + R)(R + P).
  1. Vieta: P + Q + R =−1, PQ + QR + PR =−22, PQR =40⁠.
  2. (P + Q)(Q + R)(R + P)=(sum)(pairs)− product = −18
Why it worksa(x−r)(x−s) expands to ax2−a(r+s)x+ars⁠.
Watch outThe sum has a minus sign: −ab​⁠.

Try these

  1. If the product of the roots of ax2−5x+20=0 is −4, then a=
    Show the answer

    −5

    1. Product of roots =ac​⁠, so a20​=−4⁠.
    2. a =20÷(−4)= −5
  2. P, Q and R are the roots of x3−6x2+9x=0⁠. Find (P + Q + R)+(PQ + QR + PR).
    Show the answer

    15

    1. Vieta: P + Q + R =6, PQ + QR + PR =9, PQR =0⁠.
    2. Combine them: 15
  3. If x2−7x−18=0, the larger root is
    Show the answer

    9

    1. Factor: two numbers that multiply to −18 and add to 7: 9 and −2.
    2. Roots 9 and −2. Larger: 9
  4. P, Q and R are the roots of 2x3−24x2+90x−100=0⁠. Find PQR − (PQ + QR + PR).
    Show the answer

    5

    1. Vieta: P + Q + R =12, PQ + QR + PR =45, PQR =50⁠.
    2. Combine them: 5
