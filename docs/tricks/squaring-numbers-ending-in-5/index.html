Squaring numbers ending in 5

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Any number ending in 5, squared.

How to do it

  1. Take everything in front of the 5 and multiply it by the next number up.
  2. Write 25 after it: 852 → 8×9=72 → 7225.

Worked example

25×25=
  1. Take the front, 2, times one more: 2×3=6⁠.
  2. Tack on 25: 625
Why it works(10n+5)2=100n(n+1)+25⁠.
Watch outIt works for bigger fronts too: 1152 → 11×12=132 → 13225. For 7.52⁠, do 752=5625 and put back two decimal places.
