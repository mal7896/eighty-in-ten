Determinants and matrices

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: 2×2 and 3×3 determinants, or one entry of a product.

How to do it

  1. ​ac​bd​​=ad−bc⁠.
  2. Triangular 3×3⁠: multiply down the diagonal.
  3. Entry (i,j) of AB⁠: row i of A dotted with column j of B⁠.

Worked example

​4−2​−61​​=
  1. ad−bc=(4)(1)−(−6)(−2)⁠.
  2. 4−12= -8
Why it worksZeros below the diagonal wipe out every other term.
Watch outIt's ad−bc⁠, not ab−cd⁠.
