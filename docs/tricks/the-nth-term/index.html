The nth term

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: A term far out in an arithmetic or geometric sequence.

How to do it

  1. Arithmetic: first +(n−1)× difference.
  2. Geometric: first × ration−1⁠.
  3. Told one term, asked for the one before? Undo a single step. The triangular numbers grow by n at step n⁠, so if the 16th is 136, the 15th is 136−16=120⁠.

Worked example

The 34th term of −1, 3, 7, 11, … is
  1. nth term = first +(n−1)× difference.
  2. −1+33×4= 131
Why it worksGetting to term n takes n−1 steps.
Watch outIt's n−1 steps, not n⁠.

Try these

  1. The 13th term of 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, … is 41. The 12th term is
    Show the answer

    37

    1. These are the primes.
    2. It's term 12: 37
  2. The 12th term of 3, 4, 6, 9, 13, … is 69. The 11th term is
    Show the answer

    58

    1. The gaps go up by one each time (1, 2, 3, …), so term n is term n−1 plus n−1⁠.
    2. 69−11= 58
  3. The 37th term of −5, 4, 13, 22, … is
    Show the answer

    319

    1. nth term = first +(n−1)× difference.
    2. −5+36×9= 319
  4. The 11th term of 1, 8, 27, 64, 125, … is 1331. The 10th term is
    Show the answer

    1000

    1. These are the cubes.
    2. It's term 10: 1000
