Logarithms

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Evaluating logs or solving simple log equations.

How to do it

  1. logb​x asks: b to what power gives x⁠?
  2. loga+logb=logab⁠.
  3. Different bases: rewrite both as powers of one base. log4​8=23​ because 43/2=8⁠.
  4. logb​x+logb​y−logb​z=logb​zxy​⁠. Multiply and divide first, then ask what power of b it is.

Worked example

If log3​x=4, then x=
  1. Rewrite: x=34⁠.
  2. x= 81
Why it worksLogs turn multiplication into addition.
Watch outlog with no base means base 10.
