Vertex of a parabola

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: The max or min of a quadratic.

How to do it

  1. The vertex is at x=−2ab​⁠.
  2. Plug that back in for the max or min value.
  3. g(x)=af(x−p)+q moves the vertex (h,k) to (h+p, ak+q)⁠. Inside the parentheses, x−p moves right and x+p moves left.
  4. Factored form (ax−b)(cx−d)⁠: the axis is halfway between the roots, 21​(ab​+cd​)⁠.
  5. Real roots of a(x−h)2+k⁠: two if a and k have opposite signs, one if k=0⁠, none otherwise. In standard form use b2−4ac⁠; exactly one root means b2=4ac⁠.

Worked example

The minimum value of y=x2+8x+14 is
  1. The vertex is at x=−2ab​=−4⁠.
  2. Plug in: y=1(−4)2+8(−4)+14= -2
Why it worksThe vertex sits halfway between the roots.
Watch outa>0 opens up (a minimum). a<0 opens down (a maximum).
