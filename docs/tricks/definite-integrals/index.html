Definite integrals

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: A polynomial integrated between two limits, or a double integral over a rectangle.

How to do it

  1. The antiderivative of xn is n+1xn+1​⁠.
  2. Evaluate at the top limit, then subtract the value at the bottom limit.
  3. Double integrals over a rectangle: integrate one variable, then the other. For xy it splits into ∫xdx⋅∫ydy⁠.

Worked example

∫01​(−6x−1)dx=
  1. Antiderivative: −3x2−x⁠.
  2. Evaluate at 1 and 0, subtract: −4−(0)= −4
Why it worksThe fundamental theorem of calculus.
Watch outSubtract the whole bottom value, sign and all.
