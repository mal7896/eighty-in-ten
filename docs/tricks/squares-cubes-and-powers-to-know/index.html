Squares, cubes and powers to know

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Any square, square root, cube, cube root or small power.

How to do it

  1. Squares through 30 (middle school) or 50 (high school).
  2. Cubes through 12 or 20.
  3. Powers of 2 to 212⁠, of 3 to 37⁠, of 5 to 55⁠.

Worked example

28=
  1. 210=1024 is the anchor.
  2. 28= 256
Why it worksThese sit inside half of the other tricks. Knowing them cold saves seconds on every page.
Watch outFor square roots, the last digit narrows it to two choices (1 or 9, 4 or 6, 2 or 8, 3 or 7). Size decides.
