Adding and subtracting fast

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Problems 1 and 2: plain sums and differences, decimals, money, and missing numbers.

How to do it

  1. Add left to right. Start with the bigger number and add the other in pieces: 1819+517 is 1819+500=2319⁠, then +17=2336⁠.
  2. Subtract by rounding: 2617−429 is 2617−430+1=2188⁠.
  3. If the second number is bigger, find the gap and make it negative: 1918−2026=−108⁠.
  4. Decimals: line up the points and fill in zeros. 202.6−21.3−31.4 is 202.60−52.70=149.90⁠.
  5. A blank: ​−429=2025 means the blank is 2025+429=2454⁠.

Worked example

1677−767=
  1. Round 767 up to 800:1677−800=877⁠.
  2. You took away 33 too many, so add it back: 877+33= 910
Why it worksRounding to a friendly number and then fixing the difference is still exact. It just moves the awkward part to the end.
Watch outThese open every test. A slip here costs 9 points (the 5 you didn't get plus the 4 you lose), so check the last digit before you move on.
