Limits

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: Limits at infinity, and 00​ limits that factor.

How to do it

  1. At infinity with equal degrees: the ratio of the leading coefficients.
  2. 00​ at x=a⁠: factor, cancel, then plug in.

Worked example

x→∞lim​8x2+x+84x2+3x+3​=
  1. Same degree on top and bottom: the limit is the ratio of leading coefficients.
  2. 4/8= 21​
Why it worksFar out, the highest powers drown out everything else.
Watch outIf the top's degree is smaller, the limit at infinity is 0.

Try these

  1. x→−3lim​x+3x2+2x−3​=
    Show the answer

    −4

    1. Factor the top: (x+3)(x−1)⁠. Cancel the (x+3)⁠.
    2. Plug in x=−3:−3−1= -4
  2. x→3lim​x−3x2−4x+3​=
    Show the answer

    2

    1. Factor the top: (x−3)(x−1)⁠. Cancel the (x−3)⁠.
    2. Plug in x=3:3−1= 2
  3. x→−3lim​x+3x2+3x​=
    Show the answer

    −3

    1. Factor the top: (x+3)(x+0)⁠. Cancel the (x+3)⁠.
    2. Plug in x=−3:−3+0= -3
  4. x→∞lim​8x2+52x2+4x+3​=
    Show the answer

    41​ or .25

    1. Same degree on top and bottom: the limit is the ratio of leading coefficients.
    2. 2/8= 41​
