Binomial expansions

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: A coefficient, the sum of the coefficients, or the number of terms.

How to do it

  1. The xk term of (ax+b)n is (kn​)akbn−k⁠.
  2. Sum of the coefficients: plug in x=1⁠.
  3. Number of terms: n+1⁠.
  4. (ax+b)2=Ax2+Bx+C⁠: A+B+C is the value at x=1⁠, which is (a+b)2⁠. A−B+C is the value at x=−1⁠.

Worked example

The coefficient of the 4th term in the expansion of (2x+3y)4 is
  1. Term 4 uses (34​)=4, with x to the 1 and y to the 3.
  2. 4×21×(3)3= 216
Why it worksEach term chooses which factors supply the x⁠.
Watch outKeep the sign of b when you raise it to a power.
