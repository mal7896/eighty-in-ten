Dividing by 9

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: A number divided by 9, with the answer as a mixed number.

How to do it

  1. Write running digit sums from the left: 3211 gives 3, 5, 6, 7.
  2. All but the last are the whole part: 356. The last sum over 9 is the fraction: 35697​⁠.
  3. If a running sum passes 9, carry into the digit before it. If the last sum is 9 or more, move the extra 9s into the whole part.

Worked example

638÷9=
  1. Write running digit sums left to right: 6, 9, 17.
  2. All but the last sum are the digits of the whole part (carry when a sum passes 9). The last sum over 9 is the fraction; if it is 9 or more, move each extra 9 into the whole part.
  3. Answer: 7098​
Why it works10=9+1⁠, so each place leaves its digit behind as remainder and carries the rest forward.
Watch outWatch the carries on long numbers. When in doubt, check the remainder with the digit-sum rule.
