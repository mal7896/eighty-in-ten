Area and perimeter

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Rectangles, triangles, trapezoids, rhombuses, circles and sectors.

How to do it

  1. Trapezoid: average base × height. Rhombus: 2d1​d2​​⁠. Square from its diagonal: 2d2​⁠.
  2. Circle: πr2⁠, circumference 2πr⁠. Sector: 360θ​⋅πr2⁠.
  3. Equilateral triangle: 43​​s2⁠.
  4. Square: the side is the square root of the area, and the diagonal is side ×2​⁠. Trapezoid median = area ÷ height. Rhombus side: the half-diagonals are legs of a right triangle.
  5. Circle with circumference kπ⁠: the radius is 2k​⁠. Isosceles trapezoid: the base angles match, and angles along a leg add to 180°.

Worked example

A rhombus has diagonals 10 and 24. Its area is
  1. Area of a rhombus: 2d1​d2​​⁠.
  2. 10×24/2= 120
Why it worksMost of these are rectangles cut apart and rearranged.
Watch outDiameter or radius? Read twice.
