Dividing by 5, 25, .25 and friends

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Division by numbers that are easy fractions: 5, 25, .25, .125, .4, 1.25 and so on.

How to do it

  1. ÷5⁠: double it, then divide by 10. 2125÷5=4250÷10=425⁠.
  2. ÷25⁠: multiply by 4, then divide by 100. 1030÷25=4120÷100=41.2⁠.
  3. Dividing by a decimal: write it as a fraction and flip it. ÷.125=×8⁠, ÷.4=×25​⁠, ÷1.25=×54​⁠.
  4. Mixed-number answers: divide, then put the remainder over the divisor. 2429÷6=40465​⁠.

Worked example

  2. 1150×4=4600, then ÷100= 46
Why it works5=210​ and 25=4100​⁠, so dividing by them is the same as multiplying by 102​ or 1004​⁠.
Watch outDividing by a number less than 1 makes the answer bigger. If yours got smaller, you multiplied.
