Vectors and polar coordinates

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: Dot products, magnitudes, and polar ↔ rectangular.

How to do it

  1. Dot product: multiply matching parts and add.
  2. Magnitude: x2+y2​⁠. Look for a triple.
  3. x=rcosθ⁠, y=rsinθ⁠.

Worked example

Let (x,y) be the rectangular coordinates for the polar coordinates (18, 150°). y=
  1. y=rsinθ=18×21​⁠.
  2. = 9
Why it worksPolar r is the same as a magnitude.
Watch outA dot product is a number, not a vector.
