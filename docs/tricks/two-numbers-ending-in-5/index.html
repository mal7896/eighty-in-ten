Two numbers ending in 5

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Both numbers end in 5, like 35×85⁠.

How to do it

  1. Multiply the fronts and add half their sum, rounding down.
  2. If the fronts add to an even number the answer ends in 25. If odd, it ends in 75.
  3. 35×85 → 3×8+5=29⁠, and 3+8 is odd → 2975.

Worked example

  1. Fronts: 2 and 7. Their sum 9 is odd, so the answer ends in 75.
  2. Front part: 2×7+ half of 9 (round down)=14+4=18⁠.
  3. Answer: 1875
Why it works(10a+5)(10b+5)=100(ab+2a+b​)+25⁠.
Watch outRound down the half before adding, then let the 75 take care of the remainder.
