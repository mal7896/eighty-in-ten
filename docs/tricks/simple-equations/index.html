Simple equations

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: One-variable linear equations.

How to do it

  1. Get the x terms on one side and the numbers on the other.
  2. Divide. With a fraction coefficient, multiply by its reciprocal.

Worked example

If 7x−3=3x+13, then x=
  1. Move x terms left, numbers right: 4x=16⁠.
  2. x= 4
Why it worksWhatever you do to one side, do to the other.
Watch outSigns flip when a term crosses the equals sign.
