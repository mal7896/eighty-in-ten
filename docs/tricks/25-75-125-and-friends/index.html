25, 75, 125 and friends

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Multiplying or dividing by 25, 50, 75, 125, 375, 625 or 875.

How to do it

  1. Think of the number as a fraction of 100 or 1000: 25=4100​⁠, 75=43​⋅100⁠, 125=81000​⁠, 375=83​⋅1000⁠.
  2. Divide the other number by the bottom of the fraction, multiply by the top.
  3. Add the zeros: 84×25=21×100=2100⁠.

Worked example

372×25=
  1. 25 is 1/4 of 100, so divide by 4 then tack on two zeros.
  2. 372÷4=93
  3. 93×100= 9300
Why it worksDividing by 4 or 8 is much easier than multiplying by 25 or 125.
Watch outKeep decimals that show up: 166÷4=41.5⁠, so 166×25=4150⁠. And ÷25 is the same as ×4÷100⁠.
