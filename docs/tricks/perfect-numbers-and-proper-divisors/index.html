Perfect numbers and proper divisors

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: "The sum of the first three perfect numbers", "The sum of the proper divisors of 48".

How to do it

  1. Proper divisors are all the divisors except the number itself. When they add up to the number, it is perfect.
  2. Perfect numbers to know: 6, 28, 496, 8128.
  3. Quick sum: get the sum of all divisors from the prime factors, then take away the number. 48=24⋅3⁠: (1+2+4+8+16)(1+3)=124⁠, and 124−48=76⁠.
  4. A prime's only proper divisor is 1.

Worked example

The third perfect number is
  1. Perfect numbers are 2p−1(2p−1) with 2p−1 prime: p=2,3,5,7 give 6, 28, 496, 8128.
  2. Number 3: 496
Why it worksEvery even perfect number is 2p−1(2p−1) where 2p−1 is prime: p=2,3,5,7 give 6, 28, 496, 8128.
Watch outLeave the number itself out of the proper divisors, but keep 1 in.
