Equilateral triangles and hexagons

  • Middle school: problems 41–60

Use it when: "The area of an equilateral triangle with side 10 is k3​⁠. k= ___"

How to do it

  1. Area =4s2​3​⁠, so k=4s2​⁠. Side 10: k=25⁠.
  2. Height =2s​3​⁠. A height of 73​ means a side of 14.
  3. From the perimeter, divide by 3 for the side first.
  4. Regular hexagon: six equilateral triangles, area 23s2​3​⁠.

Worked example

The area of an equilateral triangle with side 29 is k3​⁠. k=
  1. Area =4s2​3​⁠.
  2. k=841÷4= 21041​
Why it worksHalf an equilateral triangle is a 30°–60°–90° triangle with sides 2s​⁠, 2s​3​ and s⁠.
Watch outOdd sides give fractions: side 7 → k=449​=1241​⁠.
