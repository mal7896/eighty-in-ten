Counting and adding divisors

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: How many divisors does 72 have? What's their sum?

How to do it

  1. Prime-factor: 72=23⋅32⁠.
  2. Count: add 1 to each exponent and multiply, 4×3=12⁠.
  3. Sum: (1+2+4+8)(1+3+9)=15×13=195⁠.
  4. Proper divisors leave out the number itself: 16 has proper divisors 1, 2, 4, 8, with sum 15 and product 64.
  5. Sum of the prime divisors: each prime once. 135=33⋅5⁠, so 8.
  6. The sum of the reciprocals of the divisors is (sum of the divisors)÷n⁠. For 20: 2042​=2101​⁠.

Worked example

How many positive integral divisors does 180 have?
  1. 180=22⋅32⋅5⁠.
  2. Add 1 to each exponent and multiply: 3×3×2= 18
Why it worksEach divisor picks an exponent for every prime on its own.
Watch outFor odd divisors only, ignore the 2s.

Try these

  1. The sum of the proper divisors of 25 is
    Show the answer

    6

    1. 25=52⁠. Sum of divisors: (1+5+25)=31⁠.
    2. Proper divisors leave out 25 itself: 31−25= 6
  2. The sum of the reciprocals of all the positive divisors of 24 is
    Show the answer

    221​ or 25​ or 2.5

    1. Over a common denominator of 24, the reciprocals of the divisors become the divisors themselves: 2424+12+8+6+4+3+2+1​⁠.
    2. So it's (sum of divisors)÷24=2460​= 221​
  3. How many positive integral divisors does 120 have?
    Show the answer

    16

    1. 120=23⋅3⋅5⁠.
    2. Add 1 to each exponent and multiply: 4×2×2= 16
  4. The sum of the reciprocals of all the positive divisors of 36 is
    Show the answer

    23619​ or 3691​

    1. Over a common denominator of 36, the reciprocals of the divisors become the divisors themselves: 3636+18+12+9+6+4+3+2+1​⁠.
    2. So it's (sum of divisors)÷36=3691​= 23619​
