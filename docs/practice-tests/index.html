How the test works

Directions: Do not turn this page until the person conducting this test gives the signal to begin. This is a ten-minute test. There are 80 problems. Solve accurately and quickly as many as you can in the order in which they appear. ALL PROBLEMS ARE TO BE SOLVED MENTALLY. Make no calculations with paper and pencil. Write only the answer in the space provided at the end of each problem. Problems marked with a ( * ) require approximate integral answers; any answer to a starred problem that is within five percent of the exact answer will be scored correct; all other problems require exact answers.

Scoring runs through the last problem you attempt: +5 for each right answer and −4 for each wrong or skipped one, so a skip is never free. Every tenth problem (10, 20, … 80) is a starred estimation problem.

Sample problems: high school

  1. 316−260+18=
    Show the answer

    74

    1. Left to right: 316−260=56⁠.
    2. 56+18= 74
  2. 1530−1625=
    Show the answer

    −95

    1. The second number is bigger, so the answer is negative. Find the gap: 1625−1530=95⁠.
    2. Answer: −95
  3. The sum of the positive divisors of 40 is
    Show the answer

    90

    1. 40=23×5⁠. Sum of divisors: (1+2+4+8)×(1+5)=90⁠.
    2. = 90
  4. If 4 pints sell for $5.12, then a gallon costs $
    Show the answer

    10.24

    1. A gallon is 8 pints, and one pint costs $1.28.
    2. 8×$1.28=$10.24
  5. 720×11=
    Show the answer

    7920

    1. Write the ones digit, then add each pair of neighbors, moving left.
    2. ones: 0 → write 0
    3. tens: 0+2=2 → write 2
    4. hundreds: 2+7=9 → write 9
    5. thousands: 7 → write 7
    6. Answer: 7920
  6. 96×87=
    Show the answer

    8352

    1. 96 is 4 under 100, 87 is 13 under.
    2. Cross: 96−13=83⁠. That's the front.
    3. Gaps multiplied: 4×13=52⁠. Write it with 2 digits: 52.
    4. Answer: 8352
  7. 502−482=
    Show the answer

    196

    1. Use a2−b2=(a−b)(a+b)⁠.
    2. (50−48)×(50+48)=2×98
    3. Answer: 196
  8. DXLIII + DCCCXLII = (Arabic numeral)
    Show the answer

    1385

    1. DXLIII =543, DCCCXLII =842⁠.
    2. 543+842= 1385
  9. 72,620÷9=
    Show the answer

    806898​

    1. Write running digit sums left to right: 7, 9, 15, 17, 17.
    2. All but the last sum are the digits of the whole part (carry when a sum passes 9). The last sum over 9 is the fraction; if it is 9 or more, move each extra 9 into the whole part.
    3. Answer: 806898​
  10. 489×145=
    Show the answer

    67,360 to 74,450 (within 5% counts)

    1. Round one number, keep the other: 489×150≈73,350⁠.
    2. Anything within 5% of 70,905 counts.
  11. 2415+853=
    Show the answer

    3268

    1. Start from the bigger number and add the smaller one in pieces: 2415+800=3215⁠.
    2. 3215+53= 3268
  12. 101×400=
    Show the answer

    40,400

    1. 101=100+1, so this is 400×100 plus 400.
    2. 40,000+400= 40,400
  13. 75×36=
    Show the answer

    2700

    1. 75 is 3/4 of 100, so divide by 4, times 3, then tack on two zeros.
    2. 36×3÷4=27
    3. 27×100= 2700
  14. The arithmetic mean of 17, 9, 26, 30 and 00000000​ is 30.
    Show the answer

    68

    1. 5 numbers with mean 30 add up to 5×30=150⁠.
    2. 150−(17+9+26+30)= 68
  15. 81​ of 2 yards 2 feet = feet
    Show the answer

    1

    1. Convert to feet first: 2×3+2=8 feet.
    2. 81​ of 8= 1
  16. 282+28=
    Show the answer

    812

    1. 282+28=28×(28+1)=28×29⁠.
    2. Answer: 812
  17. The sum of the LCM and the GCD of 18 and 12 is
    Show the answer

    42

    1. GCD(18,12)=6⁠. LCM =18×12÷6=36⁠.
    2. 36+6= 42
  18. 82,572÷9 has a remainder of
    Show the answer

    6

    1. Add the digits: 8+2+5+7+2=24⁠.
    2. 24÷9 leaves 6
  19. If 1 gram =.04 oz, then 3.2 oz = grams
    Show the answer

    80

    1. Each gram is .04 oz, so divide: 3.2÷.04=3.2×25⁠.
    2. = 80 grams
  20. 520×810=
    Show the answer

    400,140 to 442,260 (within 5% counts)

    1. Round one number, keep the other: 520×810≈421,200⁠.
    2. Anything within 5% of 421,200 counts.

Sample problems: middle school

  1. 316−260+18=
    Show the answer

    74

    1. Left to right: 316−260=56⁠.
    2. 56+18= 74
  2. 1530−1625=
    Show the answer

    −95

    1. The second number is bigger, so the answer is negative. Find the gap: 1625−1530=95⁠.
    2. Answer: −95
  3. 6760÷5=
    Show the answer

    1352

    1. Dividing by 5 is the same as doubling and dividing by 10.
    2. 6760×2=13,520, then ÷10= 1352
  4. 9876×9+4=
    Show the answer

    88,888

    1. The pattern: 9×9+7=88,98×9+6=888,987×9+5=8888⁠. Each step down adds an 8.
    2. 4 digits → 5 eights: 88,888
  5. 11×80=
    Show the answer

    880

    1. Write the ones digit, then add each pair of neighbors, moving left.
    2. ones: 0 → write 0
    3. tens: 0+8=8 → write 8
    4. hundreds: 8 → write 8
    5. Answer: 880
  6. 95×96=
    Show the answer

    9120

    1. 95 is 5 under 100, 96 is 4 under.
    2. Cross: 95−4=91⁠. That's the front.
    3. Gaps multiplied: 5×4=20⁠. Write it with 2 digits: 20.
    4. Answer: 9120
  7. 272+27=
    Show the answer

    756

    1. 272+27=27×(27+1)=27×28⁠.
    2. Answer: 756
  8. The sum of the primes between 30 and 50 is
    Show the answer

    199

    1. Primes: 31, 37, 41, 43, 47.
    2. Sum: 199
  9. MMCLI + CLXII = (Arabic numeral)
    Show the answer

    2313

    1. MMCLI =2151, CLXII =162⁠.
    2. 2151+162= 2313
  10. 822×90=
    Show the answer

    70,281 to 77,679 (within 5% counts)

    1. Round one number, keep the other: 822×90≈73,980⁠.
    2. Anything within 5% of 73,980 counts.
  11. 272=
    Show the answer

    729

    1. Memorize squares through 30. If you blank: round to a friendly number and adjust.
    2. 272= 729
  12. 32×15=
    Show the answer

    480

    1. Double 15 → 30. Halve 32 → 16.
    2. 30×16= 480
  13. 25×77=
    Show the answer

    1925

    1. 25 is 1/4 of 100, so divide by 4 then tack on two zeros.
    2. 77÷4=19.25
    3. 19.25×100= 1925
  14. 98×4+8=
    Show the answer

    400

    1. 8=4×2, so both parts have a factor of 4:4×(98+2)⁠.
    2. 4×100= 400
  15. The sum of the median, the mode and the range of 1, 2, 1, 1, 5, 5, 1, 1 is
    Show the answer

    6

    1. In order: 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 5, 5.
    2. Median 1+ mode 1+ range 4= 6
  16. 101×19=
    Show the answer

    1919

    1. A two-digit number times 101 just repeats: 1919.
    2. Answer: 1919
  17. 23.1+84.1=
    Show the answer

    107.2

    1. Line up the decimal points (fill in zeros): 23.1 and 84.1.
    2. 23.1+84.1= 107.2
  18. 20% of 115=
    Show the answer

    23

    1. 20%=1/5⁠.
    2. 115÷5×1= 23
  19. 303%=
    Show the answer

    3.03

    1. Percent means "out of 100", so divide by 100: move the decimal point two places left.
    2. 303%=303÷100= 3.03
  20. 2643÷16=
    Show the answer

    157 to 173 (within 5% counts)

    1. How many 16s fit? 16×165=2640⁠.
    2. ≈165
