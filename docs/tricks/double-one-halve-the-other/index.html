Double one, halve the other

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: One factor ends in 5 (15, 35, 45), or one is an even number in the teens.

How to do it

  1. Double the number ending in 5 so it becomes a multiple of 10.
  2. Halve the other one.
  3. 15×78=30×39=1170⁠.

Worked example

68×12=
  1. Halve 12 → 6. Double 68 → 136.
  2. 6×136= 816
Why it worksa×b=2a×2b​⁠. The product stays the same while one factor gets rounder.
Watch outHalve only an even number, or you'll be multiplying decimals.
