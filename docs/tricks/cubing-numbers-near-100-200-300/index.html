Cubing numbers near 100, 200, 300

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: Cubes like 3023 or 5013⁠.

How to do it

  1. Split it: 302=300+2⁠, so use a=3 (the hundreds) and b=2⁠.
  2. Find four pieces: a3=27⁠, 3a2b=54⁠, 3ab2=36⁠, b3=8⁠.
  3. Each piece takes two places: 27∣54∣36∣08, so 3023=27,543,608⁠.
  4. If a piece has three digits, carry its first digit into the piece on its left.

Worked example

1043=
  1. (a+b)3=a3+3a2b+3ab2+b3 with a=100 and b=4⁠.
  2. Each piece fills two places: 1∣12∣48∣64⁠.
  3. 1⋅106+12⋅104+48⋅102+64= 1,124,864
Why it works(a+b)3=a3+3a2b+3ab2+b3⁠. With a in the hundreds, each term sits two places to the right of the one before.
Watch outWrite every piece as two digits: 13 is 01, so 2013=8,120,601⁠.
