Units digit of a big power

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: The units digit of something like 723⁠.

How to do it

  1. Only the last digit of the base matters.
  2. Its powers cycle. For 7: 7, 9, 3, 1.
  3. Divide the exponent by the cycle length. The remainder picks the spot (remainder 0 means the last one).

Worked example

The units digit of 957 is
  1. Units digits of powers of 9 cycle: 9, 1 (length 2).
  2. 57÷2 leaves 1, so take position 1 in the cycle.
  3. Units digit: 9
Why it worksUnits digits of a product depend only on the units digits.
Watch out0, 1, 5 and 6 never change. 4 and 9 cycle every 2.
