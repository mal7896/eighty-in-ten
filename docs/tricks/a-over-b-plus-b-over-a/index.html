ba​+ab​

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: A fraction plus its flip, like 75​+57​⁠.

How to do it

  1. The answer is 2+ab(a−b)2​⁠.
  2. 75​+57​=2+354​=2354​⁠.
  3. Backwards: if ba​+ab​=27849​⁠, then ab=78 and (a−b)2=49⁠, so a=13 and b=6⁠.
