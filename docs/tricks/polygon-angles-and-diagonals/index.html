Polygon angles and diagonals

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Regular polygon angles, angle sums and diagonals.

How to do it

  1. Exterior angles add to 360°. Each one is n360​⁠, and interior =180− exterior.
  2. Interior angle sum: (n−2)×180⁠.
  3. Diagonals: 2n(n−3)​⁠.
  4. From one vertex, the diagonals cut an n−gon into n−2 triangles. Any three vertices make a triangle: (3n​)⁠.
  5. Given the number of diagonals D⁠: find two numbers 3 apart that multiply to 2D⁠. 44 diagonals → 11×8=88⁠, so 11 sides.
  6. Regular polygon perimeter from an exterior angle: sides =360÷ exterior, then times the side length. Each central angle is also n360​⁠.

Worked example

Each interior angle of a regular 24-gon measures °
  1. Exterior angle =360÷24=15∘⁠.
  2. Interior =180−15= 165°
Why it worksWalking around a polygon, you turn a full 360°.
Watch outGiven an interior angle, find the exterior angle first, then n=360÷ exterior.

Try these

    2. 9×8×7÷6= 84
  2. Each exterior angle of a regular 18-gon measures °
    Show the answer

    20

    1. Exterior angles always total 360°.
    2. 360÷18= 20°
  3. A convex polygon with 8 sides has how many diagonals?
    Show the answer

    20

    1. n(n−3)/2=8×5/2⁠.
    2. = 20
  4. The sum of the interior angles of an 8-sided polygon is °
    Show the answer

    1080

    1. (n−2)×180=6×180⁠.
    2. = 1080°
