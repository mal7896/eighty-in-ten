Complex numbers

  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Powers of i⁠, products, and absolute value.

How to do it

  1. i,−1,−i,1 repeat. Use the exponent's remainder when divided by 4.
  2. Multiply like FOIL and replace i2 with −1⁠.
  3. ∣a+bi∣=a2+b2​⁠. Look for a Pythagorean triple.

Worked example

∣−5+12i∣=
  1. ∣a+bi∣=a2+b2​=√(25+144)⁠.
  2. = 13
Why it worksi2=−1⁠.
Watch out(a+bi)(a−bi)=a2+b2⁠, a real number.

    Show the answer

    1. FOIL, then i2=−1⁠.
    2. Real part: (−3)(1)−(−4)(−3)=−15⁠. Imaginary part: (−3)(−3)+(−4)(1)=5⁠.
    3. Answer: −15+5i
