a2+(ka)2

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: Two squares where one number is 2, 3, 7 or 10 times the other, like 132+392⁠.

How to do it

  1. Factor out a2⁠: you get (1+k2)a2⁠.
  2. k=2 gives 5a2⁠, k=3 gives 10a2⁠, k=7 gives 50a2⁠, k=10 gives 101a2⁠.
  3. 132+392=10×169=1690⁠.

Worked example

572+192=
  1. 57 is 3×19, so 192+(3⋅19)2=(1+9)⋅192⁠.
  2. 10×361= 3610
Why it worksa2+k2a2=(1+k2)a2⁠.
Watch outCheck the ratio is exact before you use it.
