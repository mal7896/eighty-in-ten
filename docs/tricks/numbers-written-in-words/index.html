Numbers written in words

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: "Write two and three-fifths million seventy thousand eleven in digits."

How to do it

  1. Turn the fraction of a million into digits first: 53​ of a million is 600,000, so two and three-fifths million is 2,600,000.
  2. Know the common ones: a half is 500,000, a fourth 250,000, a fifth 200,000, an eighth 125,000, three-eighths 375,000.
  3. Then add the rest: seventy thousand eleven is 70,011, so the total is 2,670,011.

Worked example

Two and five-eighths million plus four hundred twenty-six is
  1. Two and five-eighths million =2×1,000,000+85​×1,000,000=2,625,000⁠.
  2. Add four hundred twenty-six (426): 2,625,426
Why it worksIt's place value. The fraction of a million fills the big places and the rest is added on.
Watch outAdd, don't just write the pieces side by side: four and an eighth million plus nine thousand is 4,125,000+9,000=4,134,000⁠.

    Show the answer

    2. Add fifty thousand six hundred seventy-six (50,676): 9,450,676

    Show the answer

    7,189,558

    1. Seven and an eighth million =7×1,000,000+81​×1,000,000=7,125,000⁠.
    2. Add sixty-four thousand five hundred fifty-eight (64,558): 7,189,558
