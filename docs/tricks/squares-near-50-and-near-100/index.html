Squares near 50 and near 100

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Squaring a number from 41 to 59, or close to 100.

How to do it

  1. Near 50: the front is 25± (distance from 50), the back is the distance squared. 572 → 25+7=32⁠, 72=49 → 3249.
  2. Near 100: the front is the number ± its distance, the back is the distance squared. 962 → 96−4=92⁠, 42=16 → 9216.
  3. Near 1000: the front is the number ± its distance, the back three digits are the distance squared. 9932⁠: 993−7=986⁠, 72=049 → 986,049. Near 500 the front is 250± the distance: 5092=259,081⁠.

Worked example

562=
  1. 56 is 6 above 50.
  2. Front: 25+6=31⁠.
  3. Back: 62=36⁠.
  4. Answer: 3136
Why it works(50+k)2=100(25+k)+k2 and (100+k)2=100(100+2k)+k2⁠.
Watch outThe back is always two digits: 532=28∣09⁠. If the distance is 10 or more, carry: 1122=12544⁠.
