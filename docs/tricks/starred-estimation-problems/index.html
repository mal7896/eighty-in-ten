Starred estimation problems

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Every 10th problem has a star. Anything within 5% of the exact answer counts.

How to do it

  1. Round one number and keep the other exact, so the errors don't stack.
  2. Spot fractions of powers of 10: 8333≈65​⋅10000⁠, 142.857≈71​⋅1000⁠, 87.5%=87​⁠.
  3. Get the size right. 249 for an answer near 249,000 is wrong.
  4. Geometry stars: π≈3.1416⁠, 2​≈1.414⁠, 3​≈1.732⁠. Gallons to cubic inches: ×231⁠. Square miles to acres: ×640⁠. 60 mph =88 ft/s.
  5. Fourth roots: find the fourth powers on either side. 74=2401 and 84=4096⁠, so 43000​≈7.4⁠.

Worked example

* 238×94÷32=
  1. Look for cancelling first: 94/32≈2.94⁠.
  2. 238×2.94≈699

Answer: 665 to 734

Why it works5% is a lot of room. Two good significant digits almost always land inside it.
Watch outCount the digits of your answer before you write it.
