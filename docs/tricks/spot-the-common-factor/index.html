Spot the common factor

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Problems like 1996×4+16 that work out to a round number.

How to do it

  1. Look at the small number on its own. Is it a multiple of the factor? 16=4×4⁠.
  2. Pull the factor out: 1996×4+4×4=4(1996+4)⁠.
  3. Now it's round: 4×2000=8000⁠.

Worked example

3×1497+9=
  1. 9=3×3, so both parts have a factor of 3:3×(1497+3)⁠.
  2. 3×1500= 4500
Why it worksThe distributive law, ab+ac=a(b+c)⁠. The test writer picks the numbers so b+c comes out round.
Watch outCheck which way it goes. With a minus: 1505×5−25=5(1505−5)=7500⁠.
