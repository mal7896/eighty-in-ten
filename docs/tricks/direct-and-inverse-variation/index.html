Direct and inverse variation

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: "y varies directly (or inversely) as x".

How to do it

  1. Direct: xy​ stays the same. Scale y like x⁠.
  2. Inverse: xy stays the same. Divide the product by the new x⁠.

Worked example

y varies inversely as x⁠. If y=12 when x=5, then when x=8,y=
  1. Inverse: xy stays the same: 5×12=60⁠.
  2. y=60÷8= 721​
Why it worksy=kx or y=xk​⁠.
