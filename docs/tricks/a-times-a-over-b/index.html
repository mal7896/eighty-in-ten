a×ba​

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: A whole number times a fraction with the same number on top, like 11×1311​⁠.

How to do it

  1. Find the gap d= top − bottom (negative when the bottom is bigger). Here d=−2⁠.
  2. Whole part: a+d=9⁠.
  3. Fraction: bd2​=134​⁠. Answer: 9134​⁠.

Worked example

22×2322​=
  1. 23 is 1 more than 22.
  2. Whole part: 22−1=21⁠.
  3. Fraction: (gap)2 over 23=1/23⁠.
  4. Answer: 21231​
Why it worksba2​=b(b+d)2​=b+2d+bd2​⁠, and b+2d=a+d⁠.
Watch outIf d2 is bigger than the bottom, carry the extra whole into the answer.
