Percent shortcuts

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: x% of y⁠, "what percent of", and "is p% of what".

How to do it

  1. Swap if it's easier: 18% of 50 is 50% of 18, which is 9.
  2. Use the table: 37.5% of 64 is 83​ of 64, which is 24.
  3. Percent = part ÷ whole ×100⁠.
  4. x% of a is y% of what? If y is twice x⁠, the answer is half of a⁠: 19% of 518 is 38% of 259.
  5. Repeating percents are fractions: 58.3%=127​⁠, so 84 is 58.3% of 84÷127​=144⁠.

Worked example

45 is 75% of what number?
  1. 75%=3/4, so the whole is 45÷3/4=45×4/3⁠.
  2. Answer: 60
Why it works100x​⋅y=100y​⋅x⁠.
Watch outRead carefully which number is the whole.
