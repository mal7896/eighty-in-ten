Angles between clock hands

  • Middle school: problems 61–80

Use it when: "The smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 4:30 is ___°."

How to do it

  1. Angle =∣30h−5.5m∣ at h o'clock and m minutes.
  2. If that's over 180°, take it away from 360°.
  3. 4:30: ∣120−165∣=45°⁠. 9:15: ∣270−82.5∣=187.5⁠, so 360−187.5=172.5°⁠.

Worked example

The smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 3:11 is °
  1. Hour hand: 30×3+11÷2=95.5∘ past 12. Minute hand: 6×11=66∘⁠.
  2. The difference is 29.5°
Why it worksThe minute hand moves 6° a minute. The hour hand moves 30° an hour, which is half a degree a minute, so it sits at 30h+0.5m degrees past 12.
Watch outThe hour hand isn't on the hour mark: at 4:30 it's halfway between the 4 and the 5. Answers can end in .5.
