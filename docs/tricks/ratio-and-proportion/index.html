Ratio and proportion

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: "3 is to 8 as 12 is to ___".

How to do it

  1. See what the known number was multiplied by.
  2. Multiply the other number by the same amount.

Worked example

If x28​=74​⁠, then x=
  1. 28 is 7×4, so x is 7×7⁠.
  2. x= 49
Why it worksEqual ratios are scaled copies of each other.
Watch outWhen the scale isn't a whole number, cross-multiply.
