Volume and surface area

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: Boxes, cubes, cylinders, cones and spheres.

How to do it

  1. Box: lwh⁠, surface 2(lw+wh+lh)⁠. Cube: s3⁠, surface 6s2⁠.
  2. Cylinder πr2h⁠. Cone 31​πr2h⁠. Sphere 34​πr3⁠, surface 4πr2⁠.
  3. Box diagonal: l2+w2+h2​⁠.
  4. Sphere from its surface area: 4r2 is the number in front of π⁠. Regular tetrahedron with edge s⁠: surface s23​⁠, volume 12s3​2​⁠. Square pyramid: 31​s2h⁠. Cube diagonal: s3​⁠.

Worked example

A cylinder has radius 4 and height 10. Its volume is kπ⁠. k=
  1. V=πr2h⁠.
  2. k=16×10= 160
Why it worksA cone is a third of the cylinder around it.
