Multiplying mixed numbers

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: 752​×753​ (same whole number, fractions add to 1) or 881​×2481​ (same fraction).

How to do it

  1. Fractions add to 1: whole part is n(n+1)⁠, fraction part is the two fractions multiplied. 752​×753​=56256​⁠.
  2. Same fraction k1​⁠: whole × whole +(sum of wholes)/k+k21​⁠.

Worked example

365​×361​=
  1. Same whole number (3) and the fractions add to 1.
  2. Whole: 3×4=12⁠.
  3. Fraction: 5/6×1/6=365​⁠.
  4. Answer: 12365​
Why it worksBoth are just FOIL. The first one is the ending-in-5 trick for fractions.
Watch outCheck that the fractions really add to 1 before using the first shortcut.
