a2+b2−(a−b)2

  • Middle school: problems 41–60
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: Three squares where the third number is the difference of the first two: 372+252−122⁠.

How to do it

  1. The answer is just 2ab⁠: 2×37×25=1850⁠.
  2. The cousin: (a+b)2−a2−b2 is also 2ab⁠.

Worked example

692+432−262=
  1. 26=69−43, and a2+b2−(a−b)2=2ab⁠.
  2. 2×69×43= 5934
Why it works(a−b)2=a2−2ab+b2⁠, so everything cancels except 2ab⁠.
Watch outMake sure the third number really is a−b (or the first is a+b⁠).
