(n−1)!n!±(n−2)!​

  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: A factorial fraction like 7!8!−6!​⁠.

How to do it

  1. Split it: (n−1)!n!​=n and (n−1)!(n−2)!​=n−11​⁠.
  2. So the answer is n±n−11​⁠: 7!8!−6!​=8−71​=776​⁠.

Worked example

6!7!+5!​=
  1. Divide each term by 6!: 6!7!​=7 and 6!5!​=61​⁠.
  2. 7+1/6= 761​
Why it worksDividing factorials cancels everything they share.
Watch outWith the minus sign, borrow a whole: 8−71​=776​⁠.
