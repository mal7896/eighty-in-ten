Multiplying near 100

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Both numbers are close to 100 (or 1000).

How to do it

  1. Find how far each number is from 100.
  2. Front: take one number and move it by the other's distance. 97×94 → 97−6=91⁠.
  3. Back two digits: multiply the distances. 3×6=18⁠. Answer: 9118.

Worked example

87×92=
  1. 87 is 13 under 100, 92 is 8 under.
  2. Cross: 87−8=79⁠. That's the front.
  3. Gaps multiplied: 13×8=104⁠. Write it with 2 digits: 104 (carry the extra into the front).
  4. Answer: 8004
Why it works(100−a)(100−b)=100(100−a−b)+ab⁠.
Watch outThe back must be exactly two digits (98×97=95∣06⁠). If one number is over 100 and one is under, subtract instead: 103×94=9700−18=9682⁠.
