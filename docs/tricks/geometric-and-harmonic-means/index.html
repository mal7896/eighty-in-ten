Geometric and harmonic means

  • Middle school: problems 41–60

Use it when: "The harmonic mean of 2 and 3 is ___", "The geometric mean of 4, 6, and 9 is ___".

How to do it

  1. Geometric mean of two numbers: ab​⁠. Of three: 3abc​⁠. 4, 6 and 9: 3216​=6⁠.
  2. Harmonic mean of two numbers: a+b2ab​⁠. 2 and 3: 512​=252​⁠.
  3. Powers of one base: the geometric mean averages the exponents. 59,510,511,514 → 511⁠.
  4. If the root isn't whole, simplify it: 12⋅10​=120​=230​⁠.

Worked example

The harmonic mean of 11 and 14 is
  1. Harmonic mean of two numbers: a+b2ab​⁠.
  2. 252⋅11⋅14​= 12258​
Why it worksThe geometric mean is the side of the square (or cube) with the same area (or volume) as the numbers multiplied together. The harmonic mean is the reciprocal of the average reciprocal.
Watch outFor two different positive numbers, harmonic < geometric < arithmetic. Use it as a check.
