Order of operations

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: An expression mixing +⁠, −⁠, ×⁠, ÷ and powers.

How to do it

  1. Parentheses, then exponents.
  2. Multiply and divide left to right.
  3. Add and subtract left to right.

Worked example

(6−7)2×3−6=
  1. Parentheses: 6−7=−1⁠. Square: 1.
  2. 1×3=3, minus 6= −3
Why it worksMultiplication and division have the same rank. So do addition and subtraction.
Watch out12÷4×3=9⁠, not 1.
