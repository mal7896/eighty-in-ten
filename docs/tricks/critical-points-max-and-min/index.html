Critical points, max and min

  • High school: problems 61–80

Use it when: Where a cubic has a local max or min, and its value there.

How to do it

  1. Set f′(x)=0⁠.
  2. For a cubic with a positive leading coefficient, the left critical point is the max and the right one is the min.

Worked example

f(x)=x3−48x+2 has a local maximum at x=
  1. f′(x)=3(x2−16)=0 at x=±4⁠.
  2. Cubic with positive leading coefficient: max on the left, min on the right. x= -4
Why it worksThe slope changes sign at a turning point.
Watch outThey may ask for the x−value or the y−value.
