The fraction–percent table

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Converting eighths, sixths, ninths, elevenths, twelfths, sixteenths and sevenths.

How to do it

  1. Learn the unit fractions: 81​=12.5%⁠, 61​=1632​%⁠, 91​=1191​%⁠, 111​=9111​%⁠, 121​=831​%⁠, 161​=6.25%⁠, 71​=1472​%⁠.
  2. Multiply by the numerator: 85​=5×12.5%=62.5%⁠.
  3. For 40a​ or 80a​⁠, divide by 4 or 8 and move the decimal one more place.
  4. Percents with fractions in them: divide by 100. 441​%=.0425⁠, and 1221​%=81​⁠.
