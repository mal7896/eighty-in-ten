A fraction minus its near-copy

  • Middle school: problems 61–80
  • High school: problems 41–60

Use it when: Like 76​−3629​⁠, where 36=5⋅7+1 and 29=5⋅6−1⁠.

How to do it

  1. If the second is nb+1na−1​⁠, the answer is b(nb+1)a+b​⁠: 25213​⁠.
  2. If it's nb−1na+1​⁠, the answer is −b(nb−1)a+b​⁠.
  3. Reduce if you can.

Worked example

107​−2922​=
  1. 29 is 3×10−1 and 22 is 3×7+1⁠. Cross-multiplying gives 7×29−10×22=−(7+10)⁠.
  2. So the answer is −17/(10×29)=−17/290⁠.
  3. Reduced: −29017​
Why it worksCross-multiplying gives a(nb+1)−b(na−1)=a+b on top.
Watch outCheck which way the ±1s go before you pick the sign.
