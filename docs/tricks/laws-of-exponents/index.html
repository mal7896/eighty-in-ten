Laws of exponents

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: Same-base products and quotients, fractional and negative exponents.

How to do it

  1. Multiply: add the exponents. Divide: subtract.
  2. bm/n⁠: take the nth root, then raise it to the m⁠.
  3. A negative exponent means reciprocal.
  4. Factor out the smaller power: 58−57=57(5−1)=4⋅57⁠.
  5. Digits in 2a⋅5b⁠: pair the 2s and 5s into 10s. 212⋅510=4⋅1010 has 11 digits, and its digits add to 4.
