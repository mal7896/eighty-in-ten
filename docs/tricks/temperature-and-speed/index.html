Temperature and speed

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 21–40

Use it when: °C ↔ °F, and miles per hour ↔ feet per second.

How to do it

  1. F=59​C+32⁠. Quick way: double the Celsius, take off a tenth of that, add 32.
  2. C=95​(F−32)⁠.
  3. 15 mph =22 ft/s. So 30 mph is 44 ft/s and 60 mph is 88 ft/s.

Worked example

88 feet per second = miles per hour
  1. 22 ft/s =15 mph.
  2. 88 ft/s =4×15= 60 mph
Why it works1 mph is 36005280​=1522​ ft/s.
Watch out−40 is the same in both scales.
