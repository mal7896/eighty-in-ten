Equally spaced around a middle

  • Middle school: problems 21–40
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: Two numbers the same distance from an easy middle, like 83×87 (middle 85).

How to do it

  1. Square the middle.
  2. Subtract the distance squared: 83×87=852−22=7225−4=7221⁠.

Worked example

33×37=
  1. 33 and 37 are both 2 away from 35.
  2. So the product is 352−22=1225−4⁠.
  3. Answer: 1221
Why it works(m−d)(m+d)=m2−d2⁠.
Watch outWorks with decimals too: 6.8×7.2=49−0.04=48.96⁠.
