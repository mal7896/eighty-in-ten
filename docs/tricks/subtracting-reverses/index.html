Subtracting reverses

  • Middle school: problems 1–20
  • High school: problems 1–20

Use it when: A number minus its reverse, like 812−218⁠.

How to do it

  1. Two digits: 9× (difference of the digits). 73−37=36⁠.
  2. Three digits: 99× (first − last). 812−218=99×6=594⁠.
  3. Four digits with zeros inside: 999× (first − last).

Worked example

  2. 9×(4−2)= 18

Watch outIf the smaller number comes first, the answer is negative.
